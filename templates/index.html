THE GUARDIAN'S PURPOSE

THE MISSION

ONE SCRIPTURE.
ONE GUARDIAN.
ONE WORLD TO PROTECT.

Vedha's primary mission is to protect the ninth sacred book at all costs. He must embark on a dangerous Himalayan quest, unlock his hidden potential and stop Mahabir Lama from becoming an omnipotent god.

Along the way, Super Yodha protects innocent lives, spiritual sanctuaries and ancient knowledge while preserving the balance of the universe.
